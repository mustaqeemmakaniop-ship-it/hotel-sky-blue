About Hotel Sky Blue

Comfort & Convenience in Adajan, Surat

Situated on the 4th Floor of Scarlet Mall (Opposite Kalupur Bank, Near Prime Arcade) on Anand Mahal Road, Hotel Sky Blue provides a clean, peaceful, and budget-friendly stay in the heart of Adajan, Surat.

Ideal for business travelers, families, and visitors to Surat's famous textile and diamond centers. We are conveniently located just 2.7 km from Surat city centre and 13 km (8 miles) from Surat International Airport, offering comfortable rooms, elevator access, 24-hour reception, and free parking.
